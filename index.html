    LOCAL CULTURE · MARINDUQUE

    A tradition
    you can feel.

    During Holy Week, the Moriones Festival brings masked performers into the streets in a re-enactment inspired by the story of Longinus. The masks, costumes, and processions are a distinctive part of Marinduque’s cultural life.
